01 · Principles

Five principles

Every screen, from the front-desk check-in to the member app, is judged against these. When two choices are equally valid, the one closer to the studio wins.

i

Material before colour

Grounds are plaster, panels are paper, heroes are stone. Flat colour alone is never the finished state.

ii

One clear action

One ink pill per view for the main action; every other action is a tactile paper pill.

iii

Calm density

Content starts at the top. Cells keep their natural height — balance comes from real content, never empty boxes.

iv

Pigment is precious

Full-strength tones mark dots and bars; large tiles use the softened limewash.

v

Bilingual by default

Every word exists in Spanish first and English second, warm and brief, in the tú form.

02 · Colour

Colour

Deep blue is the identity; cream, sun yellow and ink complete it. Surfaces are warm paper over plaster, and seven class tones name the practices — at full pigment for marks, as limewash for tiles.

04 · Materials

Materials

Pulso Materia sits behind content, never on top of text, and keeps every ground within a few percent of its flat colour — so the room is felt and the words stay sharp. Dark mode trades the photographs for procedural stone.

Voice

Tu clase empieza en 20 minutos.
  • Spanish first, tú form, warm and brief.
  • Sentence case; eyebrows are the only capitals.
  • Colombian numbers: $1.284.000 · 07:00.

Motion

  • Controls answer in 120 ms; cards lift in 200 ms.
  • Tiles reveal gently as you arrive; nothing loops but light and breath.
  • All of it rests when reduced motion is on.

Accessibility

  • Text 4.5 : 1 or better in light and dark; limewash tiles 6.5–11.7 : 1.
  • A solid 2 px focus ring on every control.
  • 44 px targets; status always has a word or an icon.
  • Scales from a phone to a 4K reception screen.
