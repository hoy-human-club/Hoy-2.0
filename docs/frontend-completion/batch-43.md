5. **Atomic result.** One detached mock transaction: fresh eligibility/review/ownership checks, booking status+timestamp, optional exact linked return, provider-derived occupancy validation, supported waitlist effect and safe nonce/fingerprint outcome receipt/audit. Cancellation or lost acknowledgement after commit is honestly unconfirmed and never described as a rollback. Validate its tenant/session/status/shape and deterministic candidate without reading profiles or exposing another user's details. No candidate means no offer. Keep started/completed/cancelled class cancellation behavior separate from new booking: preserve the customer's allowed late cancellation while **skipping** an unclaimable new offer. Do not expire existing offers, claim/rebook, alter waitlist order/hold/capacity policy, modify no_show accounting or send notifications. Ambiguous candidate data should fail the supported operation honestly; scope outside those clear cases remains unverified. On receipt replay, an offer may legitimately have become claimed/expired/left; validate identity/scope and historical command effect without reoffering or inventing a status-transition policy.

Scoped loading/error/retry, verified/free package versus nonpackage/late/unverifiable/stale previews, immediate ref busy latch, immutable retained command for retry, stop/unmount/actor/provider fencing and truthful commit/ack states.
