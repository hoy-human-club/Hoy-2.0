## Behavior and boundaries

`src/data/trialEligibility.ts` shares one pure assessment and fresh mock-only assertion across offers and staged commands. All returned histories are filtered again by exact user and tenant, even when a provider ignores query scope; the requested account must match that tenant. Existing once-only history remains: any trial booking or another approved trial payment blocks a second trial, without reinterpreting cancelled/no-show/refund history.

A **clear confirmed active package** is a remaining original multi-class purchase with a matching approved user/tenant/plan payment, current valid expiry (inclusive in studio time), and no current freeze. Unresolved history takes precedence over package classification; no legacy fallback is used to certify trial eligibility. The lot reconstruction, original quantity, allocated debits, purchased expiry and freeze dates are read without modification. Definite exhausted/valid-expired matched-payment purchases with proven allocation and a linked approved prepaid single alone do not establish an active multi-class package. Pending/declined payment-backed grants do not activate a package.

Other relevant proof is **unresolved**, not production eligible/ineligible. Frozen lots; unlinked historical purchases; unclassified gift/adjustment grants; missing/mismatched payment provenance; malformed history/freeze; missing expiry on a remaining multi-class lot; unlinked/dangling/conflicting allocation or restoration references; or future-dated allocation/history visibly pause fictional trial testing. Historical provenance remains unresolved even when reconstruction appears spent/expired. `TRIAL_FIXTURE_SAFEGUARD` documents this mock-only pause independently of the underlying facts/reasons. No approved production frozen/legacy/gift/refund interpretation is inferred. A distinct bilingual message explains the pause; individual-class/package flows remain available under their existing rules.

C-07 `/#/app/passes` omits unavailable trials. C-04 `/#/app/checkout/:id?pass=trial` disables a forced/stale trial choice/confirm and rechecks inside its existing mock transaction before effects. S-04 `/#/staff/register` checks the selected customer, hides trial and blocks its sale/request, including a pending payment link. S-09 `/#/staff/transfers?receipt=:id` checks the current recipient before approval; a newly active package or ambiguous state leaves the request pending and all financial/booking records untouched. Explicit rejection remains available. Completed receipt decisions and existing transaction receipts replay before current eligibility, preserving idempotency after later package/history changes.

The async offer hook contains loading/read error/Retry, invalidates old user/provider/revision results synchronously, subscribes to relevant records and refreshes at studio-day rollover. Late results after actor selection changes or leaving the offer cannot restore old eligibility or produce purchases/navigation. The command assertion rereads within each staged transaction; this remains a local fixture consistency guard, not backend authorization.
