# Honest balance

(screenshots of check-in, inbox, emails and marketing) Do you think these pages and all that you've just build looks good in UX friendly way?
And criticize them Please fix these in all the user role and make it balanced looking

## Response

Gave an honest critique: several layouts faked balance by stretching cards that had nothing in them, the inbox opened onto a void, the yellow notices stacked up, and the maintenance home had a duplicate tile and an empty column.

Fixed on `ui-polish/honest-balance`:
- **Notices:** quiet notes everywhere.
- **Check-in:** masonry roster groups.
- **Inbox:** a triage pane.
- **Emails:** compact notes and a single filter row.
- **Marketing:** brand references moved into the side column.
- **Role homes:** desk, admin, teacher, member and maintenance rebuilt around real content at natural heights.

Screenshots for every page and role home are in docs/screenshots/seats. See changelog 0168.
