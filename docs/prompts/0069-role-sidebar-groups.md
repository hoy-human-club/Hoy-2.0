# 0069 — Role sidebar categories

## Prompt

For Oi, especially on the sidebars that have more stuff, like the admin, super admin, and so on, organize the sidebars to make sure that they have categorized with toggles to expand and minimize the categories in the sidebar, please.

## Response

Organized the existing role-filtered navigation into bilingual Overview, Staff, Community, Content, Finance, Administration and Design system categories. Category buttons collapse and expand their links; navigation reveals the active category. Preferences are scoped to the user, effective role and surface. The mobile drawer always shows categories even when the desktop icon rail is selected. No routes or permissions changed.
