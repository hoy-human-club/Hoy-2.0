# Buttons and tones

I think some buttons lost their shadow and style
I feel like the colors dont look great. yellow orange green

## Response

On `ui-polish/buttons-tones`:
- **Buttons:** secondary buttons, chips and option tiles are tactile paper pills again (hairline edge, top highlight, soft shadow), and primary buttons have a lift.
- **Tones:** large tone tiles use the new D-01 limewash tones (`--tone-*-wash`) without stone noise.

Contrast is measured in light and dark. See changelog 0171.
