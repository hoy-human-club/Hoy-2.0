## Responsive layout (0025)
**Shell** `AppShell` (D-0006). Below 900 px: brand top bar, a content column of at most 560 px and the sticky bottom dock (Home · Schedule · History · More), exactly the phone layout. From 900 px (`useMinWidth('shell')`): a full-viewport page, the primary nav moves into the top bar (`NavBar variant="top"`), content sits in a centred `--w-app` container (75rem, growing with the `--ui` band at 1920 / 2560 / 3840) and the document scrolls. No phone bezel at any width; the bezel exists only in the hub's `DeviceFrame` simulator.

- **≥ 900 px**: both cycles (monthly and annual) sit side by side in a two-column grid and the cycle `SegmentedControl` is hidden; the Bienvenida-passes and Especiales links sit in two columns.
- **`?plan=<id>`** (from W-05 → A-02): the plan chosen on the site is preselected (outlined) and its confirmation opens at once; the cycle follows the plan. Works at every width.
- The confirmation `Drawer side="bottom"` is a centred dialog from 900 px.
- **< 900 px**: the segmented control picks one cycle, as before.

Verified 2026-09-28 at 390 · 1280 · 3840 px (ES, light) during the 0025 build; `PageSpec.checkedAt` = `[390, 1280, 3840]`. The full 360…3840 matrix is a follow-up.
The 1280 captures above predate 0025 and may still show the phone frame; the screenshot pass regenerates them.

## Logic and integrations
- Yearly = 10× monthly, shown as '2 months free'.
- Skipping is always allowed; the nudge returns on home.
- Freeze, cancel and change-plan handled in Settings → Membership with the studio's notice period.
- Prices come from the CMS and drive the app and the website at once.
- Integrations: none

## States
- Loading: card skeletons
- Existing member: current plan marked, others show 'change'
- Promotion active: badge + strike price

## Real vs mock
- Real: the page renders from the data layer (`useData()` / `useTable`) over the tables above; every write goes through the `DataProvider` so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: no external integrations; data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.
- Note: Prices only from src/tenant/pricing.ts (Modelo de Valor v3).
- Note: Wompi is simulated in src/modules/customer/payments.ts (wompiCheckout). Manual transfer/cash create pending payments the front desk settles.
