# M-18 — Marketing local draft workbench

Route: `/#/admin/marketing`; local editor deep link `?draft=<id>`. Roles reuse existing Content permissions: marketing, coordinator, admin and super_admin. OT-01 retains original **After Nov 30**, Low, Marketing, Not verified. Exact criterion: “Campaigns, brand assets and content calendar.”

The fictional frontend stores campaign/content drafts per tenant, actor and effective role in this browser. Campaigns have bilingual titles/notes and optional planning range; content has bilingual titles/copy, optional campaign link, planning day and plain channel note. Every item stays a local draft. Preview, save, cancel and confirmed local discard never publish or send. The calendar uses strict Gregorian YYYY-MM-DD labels and UTC month math; dates never execute a task. Undated items remain visible. Campaign planning ranges do not silently assign or constrain content dates.

Brand references use only existing `brand/hoy-blue.png`, `hoy-cream.png`, `hoy-wordmark.svg` and `hoy-yellow.png`, storing IDs only. No CMS/private/local photo projection, uploads, downloads, art mutation, rights confirmation or final-art decision. Aleja final art and owner publication/approval/rights verification remain separate. Existing written image permission requirements are unchanged.

Loading/read error/retry hides partial results and editing actions. Local writes use an observed revision and SHA-256 request fingerprint, reread after hashing, and one storage document write; failed storage retains the previous document and the current editor. There is no cross-tab transactional guarantee, cross-device persistence, backend authorization or delivery service. Discard removes the row and keeps only opaque replay fingerprints. Existing provider tables are untouched.
