# W-06 — Site · Contact

## Purpose
WhatsApp, email, address, Instagram and opening hours from the tenant config — every placeholder labelled as pending — the studio location through `MapSlot` (offline-safe placeholder plus a Google Maps deep link), a WhatsApp CTA, and an "escríbenos" form that opens WhatsApp with the message prefilled. ## Real vs mock
- Real: every paragraph, class essay, tagline and value-model rationale is the owner's own copy, typed
  once in `src/tenant/brand.ts` and `src/tenant/pricing.ts` and read through `useT()` / `bi()`. Studio
  facts (city, capacity, hours, contact, location) come from `src/tenant/tenant.ts`. Schedule, teacher
  and review rows come from the data layer (`useTable`), so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: **all photography and video is pending** — every image is a `MediaSlot` that reserves the
  ratio and prints the art brief (`docs/website-vision.md` carries the shot list). Contact details:
  **WhatsApp (+57 312 776 5000) and the address (Cl. 7B Sur # 29C-100, El Poblado — the Santa María Tenis Club
  premises) are real and confirmed since 0036**; email and Instagram are still placeholders and keep the
  "Dato pendiente de confirmar" line (per-field `confirmed` in `tenant.contact` / M-08a). Data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.

## 0041 — live hours
The hours card quotes the M-08a weekly hours (not the tenant.ts constant), today's line (“Hoy: 06:00–20:00”,
“Hoy cerrado · Festivo (Día de la Raza) · abre mañana 06:00”) and up to three upcoming exceptions from M-08g. Every
website page (shared shell) also carries the studio as schema.org `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD with the same hours, and the
footer shows the sentence plus today's line.

## 0047 — WhatsApp by topic
The WhatsApp card, the "Abrir WhatsApp" CTA and the "Escríbenos" form are **`frontDesk`** handoffs; the Especiales card is a
**`specials`** handoff — all through `useWhatsappLink()` (M-08a → Contactos de WhatsApp por tema, D-0022), so a
coordination number typed there takes the Especiales conversation without touching this page. When a contact is off duty and
the studio is closed, a small line under the button says "Respondemos el próximo día hábil (…)", computed from the hours, the
M-08g exceptions and Colombia's holidays. The CTA message moved from an inline literal to the `site.contact.wa` key.
