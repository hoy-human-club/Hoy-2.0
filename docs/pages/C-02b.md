**Route** `/#/app/schedule/week` · **Roles** customer, front_desk, coordinator, super_admin · **Surface** customer · **Spec** `spec.code === 'C-02b'` (see `/#/dev/specs`)

## Purpose
The week view of C-02: a six-column day grid of compact time chips, because comparing across days is the reason to open it. Same data, filters and booking state as Today.

4. `Legend` (the visible modalities, headed “Clases / Classes”)

## Responsive layout (0026) — calendar on phones
`SessionCalendar` picks its layout from the viewport (`useMinWidth('shell')`, 900 px):

- **< 900 px · Week**: a snap-scrolling strip of the seven days (44 px chips: weekday, date, one class-tone dot per class, `aria-pressed` on the selected day) above the selected day's classes as `ClassRow`s. Tapping a chip selects the day; the toolbar arrows move a week.
- **< 900 px · Month**: a compact 7-column grid of 44 px day cells with the same dots (outside-month days dimmed, today ringed, selected day filled) above the selected day's list. Tapping a cell selects the day; arrows move a month.
- **< 900 px · Day**: unchanged (the list).
- **≥ 900 px**: the seven-column card grids of 0025, unchanged.
- No horizontal scroll region at any width; sizes are rem / tokens so the 4K band scales them. The footnote no longer asks to swipe.

Verified 2026-09-28 at 360 · 390 · 768 · 1280 · 1920 · 3840 px (ES and EN, light) during the 0026 build: DOM audit (`scrollWidth === innerWidth`, calendar window without horizontal overflow, no control under 44 px) plus the captures above regenerated at all six widths; `PageSpec.checkedAt` = `[360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 3840]`.

## Logic and integrations
- Tapping a chip opens C-03 for that class; booked chips are filled in the selection colour.
- Past days are read-only; the current day is pre-selected.
- Filters set on Today carry over to Week for the session; the chips and legend are the visible modalities and the chip/dot colour is the modality's class tone (0039).
- Six columns, Monday to Saturday — the studio does not open on Sunday; a seventh column appears only if M-08 hours change.
- Integrations: Supabase Auth, Supabase Realtime

## States
- Loading: skeleton
- Empty day: dash in the column
- Past days: chips dimmed
- Booked: chip outlined

## Real vs mock
- Real: the page renders from the data layer (`useData()` / `useTable`) over the tables above; every write goes through the `DataProvider` so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: Supabase Auth, Supabase Realtime are simulated or deferred; data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.
- Note: Rendered by SchedulePage with view=week at /app/schedule/week; on phones the six columns stack as day groups.

## Inactive classes (0066)

Calendar sessions that have started/ended, are cancelled/completed, or have unpublished templates/modalities stay visible with a status label and disabled button. Full future sessions retain waitlist navigation. Staff detail/history is unaffected.
