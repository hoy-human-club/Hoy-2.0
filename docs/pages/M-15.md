# M-15 · Promotion results

Route: `/#/admin/promotions`.

Admins and coordination inspect campaign and promoter results, separating share preparation from claims, active bookings and attended participants. Admins can pause or resume existing approved demo offers. This does not publish drafts, activate marketing or grant production permissions.

Production boundaries: [promotion backend readiness](../promotion-backend-readiness.md).
