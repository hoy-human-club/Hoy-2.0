# M-06 — CRM · member 360

**Route** `/#/admin/crm` · `/#/admin/crm/:id` · **Roles** coordinator, front_desk, super_admin, finance · **Surface** admin · **Spec** `spec.code === 'M-06'` (see `/#/dev/specs`)

## Purpose
One record per person that the whole studio trusts: who they are, what they have paid, what they have attended, and — first, on the default tab — the whole conversation with them: WhatsApp both ways, every email (newsletters, automated sends, real correspondence), the front desk's internal notes and the system's own events, in one WhatsApp-style thread with a reply box. Since 0.8.0 the thread is `message_log` (`docs/changelog/0021-crm-conversacion-y-bandeja.md`) and the same thread is what the front desk answers from in S-06.

## Sections (layout order — `useLayout(spec)`)
1. `SegmentRail (all, at risk, new, no membership, birthdays)` — `/admin/crm` list (CrmPage).
2. `MemberList (search, sort)`.
3. `MemberDetail` (`/admin/crm/:id`, MemberPage): `IdentityHeader` (avatar, plan badge, WhatsApp verified, masked phone, email, birthday; **Abrir en bandeja** → `/staff/inbox/:id` for `members.write`), `MetricRow` (LTV, visits, last visit, risk), tabs **Conversación** (default, shows the unread count) · Reservas · Pagos (if `payments.read`) · **Práctica** (0040).
4. **Conversación tab**: filter chips **Todo · WhatsApp · Email · Notas · Sistema** → `MessageThread` (the person's `message_log` rows merged by date with bookings, payments and consents as centred system lines; inbound left, outbound right, automations dashed, emails as cards with subject + Newsletter · Automático · Manual badge, internal notes as the dashed yellow card, unread inbound with a blue ring) → `MessageComposer` (WhatsApp · Email · Nota; hidden under the Sistema filter; read-only without `members.write`; WhatsApp blocked when the number is unverified; quiet-hours queue hint).
5. Reservas: `DataTable` of the member's bookings. Pagos: `DataTable` of payments.
6. **Práctica tab** (0040, read-only): `useMemberPractice(id)` — "Semanas de práctica" as a `StreakBadge` with a state line (sin meta · empezando · activa · en riesgo · se interrumpió hace poco, buen momento para escribirle), this week as `WeekDots`, tiles Clases tomadas este mes · Clases en total · Última visita (hace {n} días) · Meta ({n}/semana, "la elige el miembro en su app"), `MilestoneList`, and "Solo lectura: los mismos números que el miembro ve en su app."

## Logic and integrations
- The thread merges every channel in one chronological stream — WhatsApp, email, staff notes and system events (bookings, payments, consents) — oldest first with day separators; `isConversationRow()` excludes M-04 / M-05 test sends.
- Staff notes are `message_log` rows (`channel note`, `direction internal`), never visible to the member; health flags surface to teachers as a marker only. The audit trail keeps `member.note` without the text.
- Opening the Conversación tab calls `markConversationRead()` — every inbound row of the person gets `read_at` / `read_by`; that is what clears the bell, S-01 and S-06.
- Sending: `sendComposed()` → WhatsApp (`queued` during M-08 quiet hours, else `sent`), email (subject + body, no quiet hours) or note; `sent_by` is the signed-in user, shown as "Name · Role" under the bubble.
- Churn risk is derived from visit cadence against the member's own baseline, not a global average.
- Práctica tab: the same `practiceStats()` the member sees on C-01 / C-27, so the desk and the member never read different numbers. The segment rail's **21-day** at-risk rule (`useMemberStats`) and M-12's **14 / 30 / 60 / 90** ladder are not unified yet (ROADMAP §E 41).
- Front desk sees the timeline but cannot edit payments; Finance sees payments but not health notes.
- Every view of a member record is itself written to the activity log.
- Integrations: WhatsApp, Wompi, Email

## States
- Loading: list skeleton
- Empty segment: 'nobody matches yet'
- No consent: channels shown as unavailable
- Merged duplicate: banner with the merge history
- Conversación: unread inbound (blue ring) → read on open · empty filter ("Nada en este filtro todavía") · composer read-only (no `members.write`) · WhatsApp blocked (unverified number) · quiet hours (queued hint) · Sistema filter (events only, no composer)

## Real vs mock
- Real: the page renders from the data layer (`useData()` / `useTable`) over the tables above; every write goes through the `DataProvider` so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: WhatsApp, Wompi, Email are simulated or deferred; data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed. Every send lands in `message_log` as a real row (`sent` / `queued`) but nothing leaves the browser; inbound rows are seed until the Cloud API / email webhooks write them (ROADMAP §F 4–5).
- Note: At risk = member with plan or credits and no check-in in 21 days.
- Note: Since 0.8.0 staff notes are `message_log` rows (channel note, direction internal) — internal, never visible to the member; the old `audit_log member.note` rows are history only.
- Note: Opening a record writes member.view to audit_log (Ley 1581).
