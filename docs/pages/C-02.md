# C-02 — Class schedule

## Purpose
Find the right class fast. Two views (today, week) over one filterable list. Studio only — there are no online classes.

## Responsive layout (0025)
**Shell** `AppShell` (D-0006). Below 900 px: brand top bar, a content column of at most 560 px and the sticky bottom dock (Home · Schedule · History · More), exactly the phone layout. From 900 px (`useMinWidth('shell')`): a full-viewport page, the primary nav moves into the top bar (`NavBar variant="top"`), content sits in a centred `--w-app` container (75rem, growing with the `--ui` band at 1920 / 2560 / 3840) and the document scrolls. No phone bezel at any width; the bezel exists only in the hub's `DeviceFrame` simulator.

- **≥ 900 px**: the page uses the full `--w-app` width; the filter chips wrap onto more lines instead of scrolling sideways (the 430 px frame clipped them at 1920); the `FilterSheet` (`Drawer side="bottom"`) opens as a centred dialog.
- **< 900 px**: chips scroll horizontally with the fade mask; the filter sheet is a bottom sheet.
- Every chip and control is at least 44 px (`--h-ctl`).

Verified 2026-09-28 at 360 · 390 · 768 · 1280 · 1920 · 3840 px (ES and EN, light) during the 0026 build: DOM audit (`scrollWidth === innerWidth`, calendar window without horizontal overflow, no control under 44 px) plus the captures above regenerated at all six widths; `PageSpec.checkedAt` = `[360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 3840]`.

## Responsive layout (0026) — calendar on phones
`SessionCalendar` picks its layout from the viewport (`useMinWidth('shell')`, 900 px):

- **< 900 px · Week**: a snap-scrolling strip of the seven days (44 px chips: weekday, date, one class-tone dot per class, `aria-pressed` on the selected day) above the selected day's classes as `ClassRow`s. Tapping a chip selects the day; the toolbar arrows move a week.
- **< 900 px · Month**: a compact 7-column grid of 44 px day cells with the same dots (outside-month days dimmed, today ringed, selected day filled) above the selected day's list. Tapping a cell selects the day; arrows move a month.
- **< 900 px · Day**: unchanged (the list).
- **≥ 900 px**: the seven-column card grids of 0025, unchanged.
- No horizontal scroll region at any width; sizes are rem / tokens so the 4K band scales them. The footnote no longer asks to swipe.

- Modality chips: under 480 px of app column the chips and the Filter button wrap as one row (QA I-04); the fade-mask swipe row remains between 480 and 899 px.

## Logic and integrations
- Two views only: Today and Week. Month and recurring were removed — students book within the week, and a month grid answers a question nobody asked.
- No 'Live Online' or 'On Demand' tabs — in-studio only.
- A full class shows Waitlist; joining notifies on release with a 30-minute claim window.
- Filters persist per device for the session. The filter chips and the legend are the visible modalities (hot yoga, barre, pilates, meditación, respiración); each class row and calendar dot carries its modality's neutral colour tone (`--tone-*`, D-01 `classTones`).
- Integrations: Supabase Auth, Supabase Realtime

## States
- Loading: three skeleton rows
- Empty: 'No classes match' + clear-filters
- Error: retry
- Past days: rows read-only
- Week view (C-02b)

## Real vs mock
- Real: the page renders from the data layer (`useData()` / `useTable`) over the tables above; every write goes through the `DataProvider` so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: Supabase Auth, Supabase Realtime are simulated or deferred; data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.
- Note: C-02b week view is the Week segment of this page (?view=week).
- Note: Capacity updates live through useTable subscriptions (MockProvider change events).

## Inactive classes (0066)

Calendar sessions that have started/ended, are cancelled/completed, or have unpublished templates/modalities stay visible with a status label and disabled button. Full future sessions retain waitlist navigation. Staff detail/history is unaffected.
